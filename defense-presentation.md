Your dissertation defense is the final public presentation of your PhD. It is part celebration, part scientific presentation, and part oral exam. The goal is to demonstrate that you produced original, rigorous research and that you understand it deeply enough to defend every choice you made.

---

## What the Defense Is

A defense typically has three parts:
1. A public presentation of your research (usually 45-60 minutes)
2. Questions from the audience
3. A closed session with your committee

The public presentation is where you tell the story of your PhD. The closed session is where your committee probes the depth of your understanding.
