# Lab Research Toolkit

A collection of guides for graduate students in Rebecca's lab at Penn State. These cover the full arc of a PhD — from reading your first paper to writing your dissertation and beyond.

Start with the onboarding guides if you are new. Everything else is organized roughly by when you will need it.

---

## Guides

### Getting Started
- [PhD Onboarding](phd-onboarding.md) — What to expect and how to set yourself up for success
- [Individual Development Plan](individual-development-plan.md) — How to set goals and track your development each year
- [Email Signature](email-signature.md) — Lab email signature template and setup instructions
- [Weekly Meetings and Updates](weekly-meetings.md) — How 1-on-1s work and what Rebecca needs from you each week

### Research Skills
- [Finding and Organizing Literature](finding-literature.md) — Google Scholar, Research Rabbit, Zotero
- [How to Read a Paper](how-to-read-a-paper.md) — Active reading strategies and what to take notes on
- [Finding Data](finding-data.md) — Sources for datasets and how to evaluate them
- [AI Tools for Research](ai-tools.md) — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and how to use any of them well

### Writing
- [Writing a Research Paper](writing-a-paper.md) — The LEAP method: start with results and work outward
- [LaTeX and Overleaf](latex-and-overleaf.md) — Getting started with LaTeX for academic writing
- [Making Figures](making-figures.md) — How to make figures that are clear, honest, and publication-ready
- [Comprehensive Proposal](comprehensive-proposal.md) — How to write and structure your comprehensive exam proposal
- [Dissertation](dissertation.md) — How to write your dissertation and turn papers into chapters

### Presentations
- [Giving a Presentation](giving-a-presentation.md) — How to structure and deliver a research talk, with the lab slide template
- [Defense Presentation](defense-presentation.md) — Preparing for your dissertation defense

### Milestones
- [Committee Meetings](committee-meetings.md) — How to prepare, what to present, and how to write the report
- [Graduating](graduating.md) — Checklist and timeline for finishing your PhD

### Career
- [Git and GitHub](git-and-github.md) — Version control basics for researchers
- [Academic Job Search](academic-job-search.md) — How to find, apply for, and land an academic position
