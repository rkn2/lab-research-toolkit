## What to Include

- Full name
- Degree program and year (e.g., PhD Candidate, Civil Engineering, Year 3)
- Lab name and affiliation (Penn State)
- Email address
- Lab website or personal academic page (once you have one)
- Lab logo (from the template)

Do not include your phone number unless you have a specific reason to. Do not use informal fonts, colors, or quotes.
